What Is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a calculation that uses a person's height and weight to produce a numerical value. It is commonly used as a general screening measurement for adults.

The BMI value is calculated by comparing body weight with height. The result can then be placed into a general BMI category based on commonly used ranges.

How Is BMI Calculated?

The standard metric BMI formula uses body weight in kilograms and height in meters.

BMI Formula:

BMI = Weight in kilograms ÷ (Height in meters × Height in meters)

For example, if a person weighs 70 kilograms and is 1.75 meters tall, the calculation is:

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75)

BMI = approximately 22.9

What Does a BMI Result Mean?

BMI is a general measurement and does not provide a complete picture of an individual's health or body composition. Factors such as muscle mass, age, body composition and other individual characteristics are not included in the basic BMI formula.

A BMI result can be useful as a general reference point, but it should not be treated as a complete health assessment.
