What Is a Word Frequency Counter?

A Word Frequency Counter analyzes a piece of text and shows how many times each individual word appears. It turns a block of writing into an organized list that makes repeated words easier to identify.

The DailyTools Word Frequency Counter counts the total number of words, identifies the number of unique words and displays the highest frequency found in the text. You can also sort the results by frequency or alphabetically.

The tool treats uppercase and lowercase versions of the same word as one word, helping produce cleaner and more consistent results.

How to Use the Word Frequency Counter

  1. Enter or paste the text into the input box.
  2. Choose how you want the results to be sorted.
  3. Select the minimum number of appearances to display.
  4. Click Analyze Text to create the frequency list.
  5. Review the word counts and summary statistics.
  6. Select Copy Results if you want to copy the list.

You can edit the text and analyze it again whenever you want to generate updated results.

Understanding the Results

Total Words

Shows the total number of words found in the text, including repeated words.

Unique Words

Shows how many different words appear in the text after repeated words are combined.

Top Frequency

Shows the highest number of times any single word appears in the analyzed text.

Minimum Count

Lets you filter the results so that only words meeting your selected frequency are displayed.

Word Frequency Example

Example text:

Tools make everyday tasks easier. DailyTools makes useful tools simple.

Possible results:

tools — 2
make — 2
everyday — 1
tasks — 1
easier — 1
dailytools — 1
useful — 1
simple — 1

Punctuation does not create separate words in the results. For example, “tools,” and “tools” are counted as the same word.

How Word Frequency Is Calculated

The tool first separates the entered text into individual words. Common punctuation is removed and words are converted to a consistent lowercase format. Each matching word is then counted.

For example, the words “Example”, “example” and “EXAMPLE” are combined into one entry because they contain the same letters when case differences are ignored.

Tips for Reading Word Frequency Results

  • Start with the highest-frequency words to identify repeated terms.
  • Use alphabetical sorting to find a specific word quickly.
  • Increase the minimum count to focus on frequently repeated words.
  • Review the original text if an unexpected word appears in the results.
  • Analyze the updated version again after making changes.
  • Use the Copy Results button to save the frequency list elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the Word Frequency Counter free?

Yes. You can analyze text and view word frequency results without creating an account or paying a fee.

2. Are uppercase and lowercase words counted together?

Yes. The tool treats words with the same spelling as one entry regardless of whether they use uppercase or lowercase letters.

3. Does punctuation affect the word count?

Common punctuation around words is removed during analysis, helping the tool count matching words together.

4. What does unique words mean?

Unique words are the different words found in your text. A word that appears multiple times is counted only once in the unique word total.

5. Can I sort the results alphabetically?

Yes. Choose Alphabetical Order from the sort menu before analyzing your text.

6. Can I hide words with a low frequency?

Yes. Select a higher minimum count to display only words that appear at least the chosen number of times.

7. Can I copy the frequency results?

Yes. Select Copy Results to copy the displayed word frequency list.
