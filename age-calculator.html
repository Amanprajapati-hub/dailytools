What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates the time between a date of birth and the current date. Instead of working out the difference between dates manually, you can enter your date of birth and see your age in years, months and days.

The DailyTools Age Calculator uses calendar dates to help calculate complete years and the remaining months and days. This makes it useful when you need a more detailed result than simply knowing your age in years.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Calculating your age takes only a few simple steps:

  1. Select your date of birth using the date field.
  2. Check that the selected date is correct.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. View your age in complete years, months and days.

The calculator uses the current date when you perform the calculation, so the result updates naturally as time passes.

How Does Age Calculation Work?

Age calculation compares your date of birth with the current calendar date. The difference is then expressed as complete years, followed by the remaining months and days.

Complete Years

The calculator finds how many full birthdays have passed since your date of birth.

Remaining Months

After complete years are calculated, the remaining full calendar months are included in the result.

Remaining Days

The final value represents the remaining calendar days after complete years and months are counted.

Calendar Dates

The calculation uses actual calendar months, which can have different numbers of days.

The exact result depends on both the selected date of birth and the current date when the calculation is performed.

When Is an Age Calculator Useful?

Knowing an exact age can be helpful in a variety of everyday situations. Some common uses include:

  • Forms and applications: Check your current age when completing personal information.
  • Education: Review general age requirements for schools, courses or activities.
  • Birthday planning: Find your exact current age before an upcoming birthday.
  • Family records: Calculate the age of family members using their dates of birth.
  • General curiosity: Find out how many complete years, months and days have passed since birth.

Important Things to Know About Age Calculation

Leap Years

A leap year has 366 days, and February contains 29 days. Calendar-based calculations take changing month lengths into account when working with dates.

Different Month Lengths

Months do not all contain the same number of days. Some have 30 days, some have 31, and February has either 28 or 29 days. This is why calendar dates are important for calculating an exact age.

Future Dates

A date of birth cannot be later than the current date. This calculator checks the selected date and displays an error if an invalid or future date is entered.
