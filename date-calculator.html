What Is a Date Calculator?

A Date Calculator is an online tool that helps you find a future or past date by adding or subtracting a specific amount of time from a starting date.

You can enter a date, choose whether to add or subtract time, enter a number and select days, weeks, months or years as the unit.

The DailyTools Date Calculator automatically calculates the resulting date and displays additional information, including the weekday, month and number of calendar days changed.

How to Use the Date Calculator

Follow these steps to calculate a new date:

  1. Select your starting date using the date field.
  2. Choose Add to Date or Subtract from Date.
  3. Enter the number of days, weeks, months or years.
  4. Select the required time unit from the dropdown.
  5. Review the calculated date and other results.
  6. Use Clear All to reset the calculator.

The result updates automatically whenever you change the date, calculation type, amount or unit. You do not need to press a separate calculate button.

Understanding the Date Calculator Results

The calculator displays four results to help you understand the calculated date.

Calculated Date

Shows the new calendar date after adding or subtracting your selected time period.

Day of Week

Displays the weekday of the calculated date, such as Monday, Tuesday or Friday.

Days Changed

Shows the actual number of calendar days between the starting date and the calculated date.

Month

Displays the name of the month in which the calculated date falls.

Adding Time to a Date

The Add to Date option moves the starting date forward by the selected amount of time.

You can add a number of days, weeks, months or years. The calculator then displays the resulting future date.

Example:

Starting Date: January 1, 2026

Calculation: Add 30 Days

Result: January 31, 2026

Subtracting Time from a Date

The Subtract from Date option moves the starting date backward by the selected amount of time.

You can use the same time units: days, weeks, months and years. The calculator will display the resulting date in the past.

Example:

Starting Date: June 15, 2026

Calculation: Subtract 10 Days

Result: June 5, 2026

Understanding Date Calculation Units

You can select from four different time units. Each unit changes the starting date in a different way.

Days

Adds or subtracts individual calendar days. For example, adding 10 days moves the date forward by 10 calendar days.

Weeks

Each week represents 7 calendar days. Adding 2 weeks moves the date forward by 14 days.

Months

Moves the date forward or backward by calendar months. Month lengths are handled automatically.

Years

Changes the calendar year while preserving the month and day whenever that date exists in the target year.

How Date Calculations Work

Date calculations depend on the selected starting date, operation, amount and time unit.

Basic Calculation:

New Date = Starting Date + Selected Time

For subtraction:

New Date = Starting Date − Selected Time

Days and weeks are calculated using calendar-day changes. Months and years use calendar-based calculations, accounting for different month lengths and leap years.

Important Notes About Date Calculations

  • Months do not all have the same number of days.
  • February has 28 days in a common year and 29 days in a leap year.
  • Adding one month to a date near the end of a month may result in the last valid day of the target month.
  • Adding weeks is equivalent to adding seven calendar days per week.
  • The result depends on the selected date and the time unit.
  • Always check the calculated date before using it in an important schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the DailyTools Date Calculator free?

Yes. The Date Calculator is free to use. You can add or subtract time from dates without creating an account.

2. Can I add days to a date?

Yes. Select Add to Date, enter the number of days and choose Days as the time unit. The calculator will display the new date.

3. Can I subtract months or years?

Yes. Choose Subtract from Date and select Months or Years. The calculator will automatically calculate the earlier date.

4. How many days are in one week?

One week contains 7 calendar days. For example, adding 3 weeks is the same as adding 21 days.

5. Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses calendar-based date calculations and automatically accounts for February 29 during leap years.

6. What happens when the target month has fewer days?

If the original day does not exist in the target month, the calculator uses the last valid day of that month. For example, January 31 plus one month becomes February 28 in a non-leap year.

7. Can I calculate dates in different years?

Yes. You can select a starting date from any supported year and add or subtract days, weeks, months or years.

8. Does the result update automatically?

Yes. The result updates whenever you change the starting date, operation, amount or time unit.
