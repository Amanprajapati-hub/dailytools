What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text commonly used when a design or layout needs text before the final written content is available. It helps fill a page with realistic-looking text without distracting readers with a meaningful message.

How Placeholder Text Helps With Layouts

Text affects the appearance of a design. The length of headings, paragraphs and other content can change spacing, alignment and the overall structure of a page.

Placeholder text gives designers and developers a way to test these visual details before final content has been written or approved.

Simple example:

A website layout may need three paragraphs to test how a content section looks. Lorem Ipsum can fill those paragraphs temporarily while the final text is still being prepared.
