What Is a GST Calculator?

A GST Calculator is a simple tool that helps you work out the Goods and Services Tax on an amount. Instead of calculating percentages manually, you can enter the amount and GST rate to see the tax amount and final total instantly.

This calculator can be used in two ways. You can add GST to a price that does not include tax, or remove GST from a tax-inclusive amount to estimate the original value before tax.

The DailyTools GST Calculator also shows the GST amount split into CGST and SGST, which can make it easier to understand how the tax amount is divided in a typical intra-state GST calculation.

How to Use the GST Calculator

You can calculate a GST-inclusive or GST-exclusive amount in a few simple steps:

  1. Choose Add GST or Remove GST.
  2. Enter the amount you want to calculate.
  3. Select the applicable GST rate.
  4. Click the Calculate GST button.
  5. Review the base amount, GST amount, CGST, SGST and final total.

Use Add GST when your entered amount does not already include tax. Use Remove GST when the entered amount is already tax-inclusive and you want to estimate the amount before GST.

Add GST vs. Remove GST

Add GST

Start with a base price and calculate the tax amount that should be added to find the final GST-inclusive total.

Remove GST

Start with a price that already includes GST and calculate the estimated base price and tax portion within that total.

Example: Adding GST

If the base amount is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%, the GST amount is ₹180. The final amount becomes ₹1,180.

Example: Removing GST

If a final price of ₹1,180 already includes 18% GST, the calculator can estimate the original amount before GST and the tax portion included in the total.

How Is GST Calculated?

The calculation depends on whether the starting amount is before GST or already includes GST.

Formula for Adding GST

GST Amount = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Base Amount + GST Amount

For example, a base amount of ₹2,000 at an 18% GST rate has a GST amount of ₹360, making the final total ₹2,360.

Formula for Removing GST

Base Amount = GST-Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

GST Amount = GST-Inclusive Amount − Base Amount

This method separates an amount that already includes GST into its estimated base value and GST component.

Understanding CGST and SGST

For a typical intra-state transaction, the GST amount may be divided equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). This calculator displays each as half of the calculated GST amount.

CGST = Total GST ÷ 2

SGST = Total GST ÷ 2

For example, if the total GST amount is ₹180, the calculation displays ₹90 as CGST and ₹90 as SGST. Actual tax treatment can depend on the type and location of a transaction, so this tool is intended for general calculation and estimation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the DailyTools GST Calculator free?

What does Add GST mean?

What does Remove GST mean?
