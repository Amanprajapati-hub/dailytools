What Is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator is an online tool that converts text, website addresses and other information into a scannable Quick Response (QR) code. A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode made up of black and white squares that store information in a machine-readable format.

People can scan QR codes using a smartphone camera or a QR code scanning application. Depending on the information stored in the code, scanning it can open a website, display text or provide access to other information.

The DailyTool-Six QR Code Generator allows you to create QR codes directly in your browser. Enter your content, choose an image size and download the generated code as a PNG image.

How to Use the QR Code Generator

Follow these simple steps to create and download your QR code.

  1. Enter your information: Type a website URL, message or other text into the input box.
  2. Choose a size: Select Small, Medium or Large depending on your requirements.
  3. Generate your QR code: Click the Generate QR Code button.
  4. Preview the result: Your generated QR code will appear below the generator.
  5. Download the image: Click Download QR Code PNG to save it to your device.
  6. Test the code: Scan the downloaded image using your smartphone to confirm that it works.

The generator provides three image sizes. Each option changes the dimensions of the generated QR code image. Choose the size according to where you plan to use it.

Small - 200 × 200

Useful for small digital displays, quick previews and compact designs.

Medium - 300 × 300

Large - 400 × 400

Choosing the Right Size

Consider the final display or printing size. Test the printed code before distributing it.

These options control the dimensions of the generated image. They do not change the amount of information entered into the QR code.

Types of Information You Can Convert into QR Codes

1. Website URLs

Convert a website address into a QR code so people can scan it and visit your webpage without manually typing the URL.

Example: https://example.com

2. Plain Text Messages

Store a short message, instruction, note or other text that someone can read after scanning the QR code.

Example: Welcome! Thank you for visiting our website.

3. Business Information

Add useful business information such as a short description, contact details or a location link. Keep the information concise and test the result before printing.

4. Educational Resources

Teachers and students can use QR codes to share links to learning materials, online resources, assignments and reference pages.

Tips for Creating Better QR Codes

  • Keep your text reasonably short, especially for QR codes used in printed materials.
  • Double-check website addresses before generating the code.
  • Choose an image size suitable for your intended display or printing purpose.
  • Maintain sufficient contrast between the QR code and its background.
  • Leave enough clear space around the QR code when placing it in a design.
  • Always scan and test the downloaded image before printing or sharing it.
  • Never encode passwords or confidential information in a QR code.
