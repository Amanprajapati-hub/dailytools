What Is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI Calculator is a financial tool that estimates the fixed monthly payment required to repay a loan over a selected period. EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment.

A typical loan repayment includes two main parts: the original loan amount and the interest charged by the lender. The monthly EMI combines these amounts into regular payments over the loan tenure.

This calculator uses the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure to estimate your monthly EMI. It also shows the estimated total interest and total amount paid during the complete repayment period.

Understanding the EMI Calculator Results

Total Interest

This shows the estimated interest paid over the complete loan tenure in addition to the original amount borrowed.

Total Payment

The total payment is the combined value of the original loan amount and the estimated total interest.

Loan Tenure

The repayment period affects both your monthly EMI and the total amount of interest paid over the life of the loan.

How Is EMI Calculated?

EMI is commonly calculated using a formula that takes the loan principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments into account.

EMI Formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly installments.

The calculator automatically converts the annual interest rate into a monthly rate and converts the loan tenure from years into the total number of monthly payments.

How Different Loan Details Affect Your EMI

Changes to the loan amount, interest rate and repayment period can change the final monthly installment. Understanding these differences can make it easier to compare possible loan options.

  • Higher loan amount: Borrowing more money generally increases the monthly EMI and total repayment amount.
  • Higher interest rate: A higher rate can increase both the monthly payment and the total interest paid.
  • Longer tenure: Extending the repayment period can reduce the monthly EMI but may increase the total interest paid.
  • Shorter tenure: A shorter repayment period may increase the monthly EMI while reducing the time during which interest accumulates.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does EMI mean?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment used to repay a loan over an agreed repayment period.

2. Is this EMI Calculator free to use?

Yes. The DailyTools EMI Calculator is free to use and can be used to estimate loan repayment amounts.

3. What information do I need to calculate an EMI?

You need the loan amount, the annual interest rate and the loan tenure. The calculator uses these three values to estimate the monthly installment.

4. Does a longer loan tenure reduce the EMI?

In many cases, spreading a loan across more monthly payments can reduce the amount of each individual installment. However, the total interest paid may increase over a longer period.

5. Are the calculator results exact?

The results are estimates based on a standard EMI formula. Actual loan payments can differ because of lender policies, fees, changing rates or other loan conditions.

6. Does the total payment include interest?

Yes. The total payment shown by this calculator is the estimated loan amount plus the estimated total interest paid over the loan tenure.
