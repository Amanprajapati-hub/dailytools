What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter is an online tool that changes a measurement from one unit into another equivalent unit. It makes it easier to understand and compare measurements without performing manual calculations.

Different countries, industries and everyday activities use different measurement systems. For example, distance may be measured in kilometers or miles, weight in kilograms or pounds, and temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

The DailyTools Unit Converter supports three measurement categories: length, weight and temperature. Select the units you need and calculate the converted value instantly.

How to Use the Unit Converter

Follow these steps to convert a measurement:

  1. Select a category: Length, Weight or Temperature.
  2. Choose the original measurement unit from the Convert From dropdown.
  3. Select the unit you want to convert into from the Convert To dropdown.
  4. Enter the numerical value in the input field.
  5. Select the Convert button to calculate the result.
  6. Read the converted value displayed below the calculator.

You can change the category or units whenever needed and perform another conversion.

Length Conversion Explained

Length measures the distance between two points. It is commonly used in construction, education, travel, manufacturing and everyday activities.

The metric system uses meters as its basic length unit, while other measurement systems commonly use feet, inches and miles.

Common Length Conversion Formulas

  • 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters
  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 1,000 millimeters
  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters
  • 1 foot = 0.3048 meters
  • 1 mile = 1,609.344 meters

Example: Convert 5 Kilometers to Meters

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters

5 × 1,000 = 5,000 meters

Therefore, 5 kilometers equals 5,000 meters.

Weight Conversion Explained

Weight measurements are commonly used when measuring food ingredients, luggage, packages, body weight and industrial materials.

Kilograms and grams are part of the metric system. Pounds and ounces are commonly used in other measurement systems.

Common Weight Conversion Formulas

  • 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams
  • 1 gram = 1,000 milligrams
  • 1 kilogram ≈ 2.20462 pounds
  • 1 pound = 16 ounces
  • 1 metric tonne = 1,000 kilograms

Example: Convert 3 Kilograms to Grams

1 kilogram = 1,000 grams

3 × 1,000 = 3,000 grams

Therefore, 3 kilograms equals 3,000 grams.

Temperature Conversion Explained

Temperature describes how hot or cold something is. Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin are three commonly used temperature scales.

Celsius is widely used in everyday life and scientific applications. Fahrenheit is used in some countries, while Kelvin is commonly used in scientific measurements.

Temperature Conversion Formulas

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • Celsius to Kelvin: °C + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Celsius: K − 273.15

Example: Convert 25°C to Fahrenheit

Formula: (25 × 9/5) + 32

Result: 77°F

Therefore, 25 degrees Celsius equals 77 degrees Fahrenheit.

Tips for Accurate Unit Conversion

  • Always select the correct measurement category before converting.
  • Check both the original and target units before entering a value.
  • Make sure the entered value uses the correct measurement unit.
  • Remember that temperature conversions use different formulas from length and weight.
  • Keep sufficient decimal precision when working with scientific or technical values.
  • Check the result's unit label to avoid confusing similar measurements.

Yes. Negative Celsius and Fahrenheit values are supported. However, temperatures below absolute zero are not valid and will display an error message.

No. Kelvin cannot be lower than absolute zero, which is 0 K. The calculator checks this condition before performing a conversion.

The calculator displays up to eight decimal places, depending on the converted value. Trailing unnecessary zeros are removed.
