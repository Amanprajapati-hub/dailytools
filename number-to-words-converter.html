What Is a Number to Words Converter?

A Number to Words Converter is an online tool that changes numerical values into their written English form. For example, the number 125 becomes "One Hundred Twenty-Five".

Writing numbers in words is useful for preparing documents, checking written amounts, educational exercises and other number-related tasks.

This DailyTools converter supports both the Indian and International numbering systems. It can also convert decimal and negative numbers.

How to Use the Number to Words Converter

  5. Click Copy Words to copy the result.

Indian and International Number Systems

Large numbers are grouped differently in the Indian and International numbering systems. Select the system that matches the format you need.

Indian Number System

Uses Thousand, Lakh, Crore, Arab and Kharab. For example, 1,00,000 is one lakh and 1,00,00,000 is one crore.

International Number System

Uses Thousand, Million, Billion, Trillion and larger groups. For example, 1,000,000 is one million.

How Number to Words Conversion Works

The converter separates a number into smaller groups based on its place values. Each group is converted into words and combined with the appropriate number scale.

Example: 1,25,450

1 Lakh = One Lakh

25 Thousand = Twenty-Five Thousand

450 = Four Hundred Fifty

Result: One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand Four Hundred Fifty

Decimal digits are read individually after the word "Point". For example, 12.45 becomes "Twelve Point Four Five".

Understanding Decimal and Negative Numbers

Decimal Numbers

Numbers containing a decimal point are converted by reading each digit after the decimal point separately. For example, 10.25 becomes Ten Point Two Five.

Negative Numbers

A negative number is converted by adding the word "Minus" before the number. For example, -25 becomes Minus Twenty-Five.

Tips for Accurate Number Conversion

  • Use commas if they make large numbers easier to read.

  • Remember that decimal digits are read separately after the decimal point.
