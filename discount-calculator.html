What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator is an online tool that helps shoppers determine how much money they can save when purchasing products with a price reduction. Instead of calculating percentages manually, you can enter the original price and discount rate to get the result instantly.

Discounts are commonly offered during seasonal sales, festival promotions, clearance events and special shopping offers. Understanding the actual reduction helps you compare prices and make more informed purchasing decisions.

The DailyTools Discount Calculator displays the original price, discount percentage, amount saved and final price after applying the discount. It uses Indian Rupees (₹) for currency formatting.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

You only need two values to calculate a discount. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Enter the original price of the product in the Original Price field.
  2. Enter the discount percentage offered by the seller or store.
  3. Select the Calculate Discount button.
  4. Review the original price and discount percentage in the result section.
  5. Check the amount you save and the final price after the discount.

The calculator accepts decimal values, so you can calculate discounts for prices that include paise or percentage offers with decimal points.

How Is a Discount Calculated?

A percentage discount represents the portion of the original price that is removed from the selling price. To calculate the discount amount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide the result by 100.

Discount Amount

Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Final Price

Original Price − Discount Amount

Calculation Example:

Original Price: ₹2,000

Discount: 20%

Discount Amount: ₹2,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹400

Final Price: ₹2,000 − ₹400 = ₹1,600

You save ₹400 and pay ₹1,600 after the discount.

Percentage Discount vs. Flat Discount

A percentage discount and a flat discount both reduce the original price, but they work differently.

Percentage Discount

A percentage discount is calculated as a proportion of the original price. For example, a 20% discount on a ₹1,000 product reduces the price by ₹200.

Flat Discount

A flat discount removes a fixed amount from the original price. For example, a flat ₹200 discount on a ₹1,000 product reduces the price to ₹800.

The calculator on this page is designed for percentage-based discounts. Flat discounts can be calculated separately by subtracting the fixed amount from the original price.

Tips for Getting the Most from Discounts

  • Compare the original price across different sellers before accepting an offer.
  • Check the final price rather than focusing only on the advertised discount percentage.
  • Review shipping charges and additional fees that may increase the amount you pay.
  • Read offer conditions to check whether a discount requires a minimum purchase.
  • Compare similar products before deciding which offer provides better value.
  • Avoid unnecessary purchases simply because a product has a large discount.
