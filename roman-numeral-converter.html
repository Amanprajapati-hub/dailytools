What Is a Roman Numeral Converter?

A Roman Numeral Converter is an online tool that changes regular numbers into Roman numerals and converts Roman numerals back into standard numbers. It makes number conversion simple without requiring manual calculations.

Roman numerals originated in ancient Rome. Instead of using digits like 1, 2 and 3, this number system uses letters such as I, V, X, L, C, D and M to represent different numerical values.

The DailyTools Roman Numeral Converter supports both conversion directions. It automatically identifies whether you enter a regular number or a Roman numeral and displays the corresponding result.

Important Roman Numeral Rules

Roman numerals follow specific rules for combining symbols. Understanding these rules helps explain how different values are represented.

  • Addition: When a smaller value comes after a larger value, the values are added. For example, VI = 6.
  • Subtraction: When a smaller value comes before a larger value, it is subtracted. For example, IV = 4.
  • Repeated symbols: I, X, C and M can generally be repeated up to three times in a row.
  • Subtractive combinations: IV, IX, XL, XC, CD and CM are standard subtractive pairs.
  • Number range: Standard Roman numeral notation in this converter supports whole numbers from 1 to 3999.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the Roman Numeral Converter free?

2. What is the largest number supported?

The converter supports standard Roman numeral values from 1 to 3999.

3. Can I convert Roman numerals into regular numbers?

Yes. Enter a valid Roman numeral such as XIV or MMXXVI, and the tool will automatically convert it into a regular number.

4. What does IV mean in Roman numerals?

IV represents 4. The smaller value I comes before V, so 1 is subtracted from 5.

5. Are lowercase Roman numerals accepted?

Yes. You can enter lowercase Roman numerals. The converter automatically changes them into uppercase before processing.

6. Is there a Roman numeral symbol for zero?

Traditional Roman numerals do not have a standard symbol for zero. This converter accepts numbers starting from 1.

7. Does the converter accept invalid Roman numerals?

No. It checks the standard Roman numeral format and rejects invalid combinations such as IIII, VV, IC and XM.

Yes. After converting a value, click Copy Result to copy it to your clipboard.
