What Is a Time Duration Calculator?

A Time Duration Calculator is an online tool that measures the amount of time between a starting time and an ending time. It converts the difference into hours, minutes and seconds.

Enter two times and click Calculate Duration to get the result. The calculator also displays the duration in decimal hours, which is useful for numerical calculations.

The DailyTools Time Duration Calculator supports time ranges that cross midnight through its next-day option.

How to Use the Time Duration Calculator

Follow these simple steps to calculate the duration between two times:

  1. Enter the starting time in the Start Time field.
  2. Enter the ending time in the End Time field.
  3. Select End time is on the next day if the time range crosses midnight.
  4. Click the Calculate Duration button.
  5. Review the total hours, minutes and seconds.
  6. Click Reset to restore the calculator to its default values.

Understanding the Four Time Duration Results

Each result represents the same time duration in a different unit.

Total Hours

Shows the complete duration converted into hours. The result can include fractional hours.

Total Minutes

Shows the complete duration in minutes. Every hour contains 60 minutes.

Total Seconds

Converts the entire duration into seconds. Every minute contains 60 seconds.

Decimal Hours

Displays the duration as a decimal number of hours. For example, 90 minutes equals 1.5 hours.

Understanding Time Duration Formulas

A time duration is calculated by subtracting the starting time from the ending time. The resulting difference can then be converted into different time units.

Basic Formula:

Duration = End Time − Start Time

Convert to Minutes:

Total Minutes = Total Seconds ÷ 60

Convert to Hours:

Total Hours = Total Seconds ÷ 3600

When the end time belongs to the next calendar day, 24 hours are added to the ending time before calculating the difference.

Understanding Decimal Hours

Decimal hours express a time duration as a number with a decimal point instead of separate hours and minutes. This makes time easier to use in mathematical calculations.

30 Minutes

30 minutes is equal to 0.5 decimal hours.

15 Minutes

15 minutes is equal to 0.25 decimal hours.

45 Minutes

45 minutes is equal to 0.75 decimal hours.

90 Minutes

90 minutes is equal to 1.5 decimal hours.

Tips for Accurate Time Calculations

  • Check that both starting and ending times are entered correctly.
  • Use the next-day option when the ending time falls after midnight.
  • Remember that one hour contains 60 minutes.
  • Remember that one minute contains 60 seconds.
  • Use decimal hours when working with numerical time calculations.
  • Click Calculate Duration after entering or changing the times.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the Time Duration Calculator free?

Yes. The DailyTools Time Duration Calculator is free to use. You can calculate time differences without creating an account.

2. Can I calculate time in hours and minutes?

Yes. Enter your starting and ending times and click Calculate Duration. The calculator displays total hours, minutes and seconds.

3. Can this calculator handle overnight time?

Yes. Enable the End time is on the next day option when your ending time falls after midnight.

4. What are decimal hours?

Decimal hours represent time as a decimal number. For example, 1 hour and 30 minutes is equal to 1.5 hours.

5. How many minutes are in one hour?

One hour contains 60 minutes. To convert hours into minutes, multiply the number of hours by 60.

6. How many seconds are in one minute?

One minute contains 60 seconds. One hour contains 3,600 seconds.

7. Do I need to click Calculate Duration?

Yes. Enter both times and click Calculate Duration to display the updated results.

8. Can I calculate a duration of zero?

Yes. If the starting and ending times are identical and the next-day option is not selected, the duration is zero.
