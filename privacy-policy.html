Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit DailyTools, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Introduction

Welcome to DailyTools. We provide free online tools designed to help visitors complete everyday tasks, including calculations, text-related activities, conversions, and other useful functions.

This policy applies to information collected through our website and explains how we handle information when you browse our pages or use our tools.

2. Information We Collect

You can use many features of DailyTools without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

Depending on how you interact with the website, information that may be collected includes:

  • Information you voluntarily provide through our contact form or other communication methods.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, device type, operating system, and general usage information.
  • Website activity, such as pages visited, approximate visit times, and interactions with website features.
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies by us or third-party services.

We do not require visitors to provide personal information simply to access the general features of our online tools.

3. Information Entered Into Our Tools

Many DailyTools features are designed to process the information you enter directly in your browser and display the result without requiring an account.

The way information is processed may vary depending on the individual tool and any external service or library it uses. Please do not enter passwords, financial details, confidential business information, or other sensitive personal information into our tools.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

DailyTools and certain third-party services may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Keeping website features working properly.
  • Understanding website traffic and visitor activity.
  • Measuring website performance and improving our services.
  • Supporting advertising, including ad delivery and measurement.
  • Remembering certain preferences where supported.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some website features or third-party services work.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

DailyTools may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising partners.

Google, as a third-party vendor, may use cookies to serve advertisements on this website. These cookies may help Google and its partners display ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

Depending on the visitor's settings, applicable consent choices, and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

6. Third-Party Services and External Links

Some website features may rely on third-party libraries, tools, advertising providers, analytics services, or external websites.

These third parties may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. DailyTools does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

When you follow an external link, we recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the website you visit.

7. How We Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our website and online tools.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Identify technical issues and improve website performance.
  • Protect the website against spam, misuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Support advertising and measure advertisement performance.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell visitors' personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the website.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect our rights, visitors, and website security.

Third-party advertising and analytics providers may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to help protect information associated with our website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, internet connection, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and the retention practices of third-party service providers.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings and manage Google's personalized advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us through our Contact page.

11. Children's Privacy

DailyTools is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12. International Visitors

Visitors may access DailyTools from different countries. Depending on your location, additional privacy protections or consent requirements may apply.

Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate notices and consent mechanisms for relevant data collection and advertising technologies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features, services, advertising practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us through our website.
